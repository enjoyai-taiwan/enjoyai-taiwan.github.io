2026 正式賽項

三個賽項都使用 EnjoyAI 官方競賽平台（鯨魚機器人專業版系列）。依孩子的年齡選擇組別。

古文明探險家

學前組、國小低年級組（5–9 歲）

By collecting civilization fragments with robots, young starchasers will trace the footsteps of history, uncovering the creative codes behind these legendary civilizations.

WhalesBot U30 pro 或 U20 pro，加裝機械夾爪

極速飛行・無人機足球

學前組至國小高年級組（5–12 歲）

Drone Cup is a dynamic team competition that integrates drone robotics, real-time communication, and intelligent decision-making.

WhalesBot Eagle 125F

星際對決・機器人對戰

國小高年級至高中組（9–18 歲）

High-stakes competitive challenge where two teams - Red and Blue - go head-to-head. Members must communicate clearly and adjust strategy.

WhalesBot AI Module 3s
